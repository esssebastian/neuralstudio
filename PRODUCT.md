# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: brands, startups, and modern companies (marketing/founder decision-makers) who want AI-generated visuals, cinematic storytelling, digital branding, and next-generation content production — reached through `index.html` / `index-en.html` / `index2.html` and the contact form.

Secondary: a Spanish-speaking audience of aspiring builders who register for the free webinar ("Cómo construir una app de $25,000 con IA en 60 minutos") and join the community via `leads.html` / `subscribe.html`. This track is lead-generation and audience-building in support of the agency, not a separate paid product.

## Product Purpose

Neural Studio is an AI-native creative studio. It exists to help brands create emotional, unforgettable experiences by blending design, storytelling, and AI production — the agency (client project work) is the core business. Success is signed client work; the webinar/community funnel exists to generate leads, authority, and audience for that agency business, not to monetize independently.

## Positioning

The differentiator is the AI-native production pipeline itself: a repeatable process (Strategy → Storytelling → AI Production → Human Experience, per `index.html`'s "How we work") that produces high-quality AI-generated visuals, video, and branding faster and with more emotional craft than a conventional studio or a generic AI-tool user. The mechanism, not any one person, is the claim a competing studio couldn't truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

- Client-facing agency site (English): hero, "What we create" services grid, 4-step process, contact form (recently gained international phone-dialing-code support).

- Spanish-language webinar funnel (`webinar.html` / `webinar-light.html`) is built on a third-party webinar/registration platform (`wk_` prefixed embed, WebinarKit or similar) with its own timer, registration form, and confirmation page (`webinar-gracias.html`).

## Product Principles

1. Human warmth over cold AI-tech framing — every surface should feel emotional and authored, not templated.
2. The agency (client work) is the business; the community/webinar funnel is in service of it, not a competing priority.
3. Don't invent proof — real client work (like the dental set) is evidence to feature; testimonials, logos, and stats stay unconfirmed until provided.
4. Preserve the third-party webinar embed's function; redesign around it rather than rebuilding its internals.
