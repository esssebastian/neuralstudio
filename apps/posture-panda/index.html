Posture Panda

MacFree

Sit up straight, without having to think about it.

Posture Panda uses your Mac's camera to follow your head, your shoulders and how close you lean to the screen, and speaks up only when your posture slips.

Download for MacRelease notes

Free · Version 1.3.0 · macOS 11+ · Apple Silicon & Intel · English, Español, Français

How it works.

  1. 1

    Calibrate once.

    Sit the way you want to sit and press a button.

  2. 2

    It watches your posture.

    It tracks your head and shoulders right on your Mac. Slouch for 8 seconds and a banner pops up.

  3. 3

    It reminds you to move.

    After 45 minutes of sitting, time to stand up and stretch.

What's inside.

Calibrate once

Sit the way you want to sit, press a button, done.

Alerts that wait

Hold a bad posture for 8 seconds and a banner appears above your windows. No nagging for a quick stretch.

Reminds you to move

After 45 minutes sitting, it tells you to stand up and stretch.

Easy on battery

Step away and the camera switches off until you are back.

Your camera never leaves your Mac

Every frame is analyzed on your computer and thrown away. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded.

Signed & notarized

Signed with our Apple Developer ID and checked by Apple, so it opens without security warnings.

Take a look.

Real screens from the app.

Private by design

Your data stays with you.

Every frame is analyzed on your Mac and thrown away. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded.

Read the privacy policy

Install in a minute

Open the downloaded .dmg, drag Posture Panda into Applications, open it and allow camera access when macOS asks.
