Calma

iPhoneParenting

Calm words for the hardest moments of parenting.

When a meltdown hits, Calma walks you through it: who needs you, what's happening, a slow breath together and the words to say. In English, Spanish or both.

Coming soon to the App StoreIn development

iPhone · English, Español or both · For parents

How it works.

  1. 1

    Who needs you?

    Pick the child who is having a hard time.

  2. 2

    What's happening?

    Choose the situation, so Calma can find the right words.

  3. 3

    Breathe, then say this.

    A short guided breath, then a few calm lines to say, in your language.

What's inside.

Right now

A full-screen flow for the moment itself: no menus, just the next step.

Words that help

A library of short scripts for common moments, in English and Spanish.

Repair

Every parent loses it sometimes. Repairing is what matters, and Calma helps you do it.

Tonight: 3 wins

A gentle nightly nudge to notice three things that went well.

Patterns

Log what happened and see what tends to set off the hard moments.

For the whole family

Share with your partner through iCloud so you're both on the same page.

Private by design

Your data stays with you.

Calma keeps your family's notes in iCloud and asks for notifications only if you turn the nightly reminder on. Calma is not medical or psychological advice.
