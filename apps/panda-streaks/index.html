How it works.

  1. 1

    Add your daily goals.

    Read, drink water, move, whatever matters to you.

  2. 2

    Check them off to feed your panda.

    Health, XP, confetti and a happy dance. Skip a day and your panda feels it.

  3. 3

    Keep the streak, watch it grow.

    From baby, to young adventurer, to a grown-up with a bamboo crown.

What's inside.

Feed your panda

Every goal you check off gives your panda health and XP. Miss a day and they get sad, then tired. Come back and they bounce right back.

Build streaks

Do a goal day after day to grow its streak. Longer streaks earn more XP, and the streak map shows every day you did it.

Watch them grow

Level up from baby, to young adventurer, to a grown-up with a bamboo crown. Pick a boy or girl panda and give them a name.

On your Home Screen

Widgets show your panda and let you check off goals without opening the app. Lock Screen widgets keep your streak in sight.

Gentle reminders

A morning "your panda is hungry" nudge, and an evening reminder only when you still have goals left.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.
