Dr. Panda

Sit up straight, without having to think about it.

Dr. Panda uses your camera and an AI pose model to track your head, shoulders and how close you lean to the screen, and speaks up only when your posture slips.

  • Calibrate once. Sit the way you want to sit, press a button, done.
  • Alerts that wait. Hold a bad posture for 8 seconds and a banner appears above your windows.
  • Reminds you to move. After 45 minutes sitting, it tells you to stand up and stretch.
  • Easy on battery. Step away and the camera switches off until you're back.

Fix My Face You're frowning / scrunching your face

Fix My Face

Unclench. Your face carries more tension than you think.

Frowning at a screen all day means sustained muscle tension, tension headaches and expression lines, and it can make you look more upset than you are. Fix My Face notices the moment it happens.

  • Learns your relaxed face. Calibrate once with a neutral expression.
  • Reads the tension. An AI face model measures lowered brows and a scrunched nose against your baseline.
  • Catches it in the moment. Hold the gesture and a banner reminds you to let go.
  • Stays out of the way. Close the window; it keeps watching from the menu bar.

Your camera never leaves your Mac.

Processed on-device

Every frame is analyzed on your computer and thrown away. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded. On launch, the apps download the open-source MediaPipe model; no camera data goes with it.

Signed & notarized

Both apps are signed with our Apple Developer ID and checked by Apple, so they open without security warnings.

Install in a minute

Open the downloaded .dmg, drag the app into Applications, open it and allow camera access when macOS asks.
