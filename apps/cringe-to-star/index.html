How it works.

  1. 1

    Pick your character.

    They start out Shy, hiding behind their phone, like most of us.

  2. 2

    Do one small mission a day.

    It starts with practice in a private camera nobody sees, and builds up to posting.

  3. 3

    Grow from Shy to Star.

    A 180-day path through Trying, Confident and Creator. We measure effort, never fame.

What's inside.

One small mission a day

180 missions that start tiny, like "record 10 seconds saying your name, then delete it", and build up to your first Story, your first video and a weekly posting rhythm. Feeling brave? Do up to two bonus missions.

Practice in private

The practice camera is just for you. Nothing is posted or uploaded, and clips are deleted unless you choose to keep them on your phone.

Watch yourself grow

Every mission adds confidence and grows your streak. Level up through five stages with your own animated character.

Hard days are okay

Skip a day and your character gets a little nervous, but you never lose your progress. Low energy? Do a "small win" instead. It still counts.

See how far you've come

A streak map of every day you showed up, minutes on camera, posts shared, and "how cringe do you feel?" check-ins that compare today with day one.

Always with you

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with today's mission, plus gentle morning and evening reminders.
