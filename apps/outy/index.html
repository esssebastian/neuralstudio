Outy

iPhonePlans

Something to do. Right when you're free.

Outy shows you the best things to do across British Columbia, filtered by when you're actually free. Swipe right to save a plan, left to skip it. No endless lists, just a deck of good ideas for tonight, this weekend or your next day off.

Coming soon to the App StoreVisit the Outy website

Free to download · iPhone · Outy Plus optional · Across British Columbia

How it works.

  1. 1

    Tell it when you are free.

    Weekdays, weekends or a specific day, and your city: Vancouver, Whistler, Victoria, Tofino, Kelowna and more.

  2. 2

    Swipe through plans.

    Right to save, left to skip. Can't decide? Shake your phone and Outy picks one for you.

  3. 3

    Go, with people.

    Turn any plan into a meetup or join one, sort out the details in the chat and see it all on the map.

What's inside.

Swipe through plans

Hikes, food spots, culture, nightlife and wellness, picked for BC. Kid-friendly plans in one tap.

Can't decide? Shake it

Shake your phone and Outy picks one plan for you from everything that fits.

Go with people

Turn any plan into a meetup, or join one someone else started. See who's going and chat.

See it on the map

Every plan on one map, so you can see what's close and what you've already done.

Unlock deals

Some plans come with a perk from the business, like a discount code you show at the door.

Climb the leaderboard

Mark a plan as visited, review it and add a photo to earn points on the monthly board.
