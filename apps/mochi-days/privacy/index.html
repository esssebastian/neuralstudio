Privacy Policy

Mochi Days · Last updated October 25, 2026

Data not collected

The short version: Mochi Days doesn't collect, upload or share any of your data. Your countdowns stay on your iPhone, and a countdown only leaves it when you choose to share it.

This policy covers the iPhone app Mochi Days, its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and the shared-countdown page at neuralst.com/mochi, made by Neural Studio ("we").

Data stored on your iPhone

To work, the app saves a small amount of information on your device only:

  • Your countdowns: the name, emoji, date, color and whether they repeat every year.
  • Which countdown your widgets show, and your reminder settings.

This information is shared only between the app and its widgets on the same iPhone. It's included in your device backups like any other app data, and deleting the app removes it. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Sharing a countdown

When you tap Share, the app creates a link that contains that one countdown's name, emoji, date, color and whether it repeats, plus a picture of it. You choose where to send it (for example Messages or WhatsApp), and it goes only to the people you send it to. The details travel inside the link itself: we don't store shared countdowns on any server.

If someone opens your link without the app, it shows the countdown on a web page at neuralst.com/mochi. That page reads the countdown from the link in their browser and doesn't save it. It has no analytics or tracking; like any website, it's delivered by our hosting provider, and it loads fonts from Google Fonts.

When someone shares a countdown with you, it's only added to your countdowns if you tap "Add to my countdowns".

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the reminders (a week before, the day before and on the day) are scheduled on your iPhone itself. No server is involved. You can change them in the app (Settings → Reminders) or turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Purchases

Mochi Days Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase handled entirely by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether Pro is unlocked.

No data collection

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers. It works without an internet connection (except for buying or restoring Pro). We do not collect, sell or share any personal information.

Feedback and ratings

If you choose to send feedback from inside the app, it opens your own email app with a draft addressed to us, including your rating, your message, the app version and your iOS version. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use emails we receive only to reply and to improve the app.

The "Rate Mochi Days" option uses Apple's built-in rating prompt; any rating or review you leave is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
