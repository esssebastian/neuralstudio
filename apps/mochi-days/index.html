Mochi Days

iPhoneCountdowns

Count down to the days you can't wait for, with a fluffy friend who gets excited with you.

Birthdays, trips, concerts, Christmas. Add a date and Mochi keeps count. The closer it gets, the more excited Mochi gets: calm reading a month out, bouncing the day before, and a full party on the big day.

Coming soon to the App StoreAlmost ready

iPhone · Free, with an optional Pro upgrade · No ads, no accounts

How it works.

  1. 1

    Add a date.

    A birthday, a trip or anything you can't wait for. Quick starts for birthdays, Christmas and New Year.

  2. 2

    Watch the countdown.

    Days, hours, minutes and seconds, live. Yearly dates like birthdays roll over on their own.

  3. 3

    Mochi gets excited with you.

    Calm a month out, bouncing the week before, a full party on the day.
