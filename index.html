Panda Streaks

iPhoneHabits

Your habits keep a little panda alive. No pressure.

How it works

  1. Add your daily goals. Read, drink water, move, whatever matters to you.
  2. Check them off to feed your panda. Health, XP, confetti and a happy dance. Skip a day and your panda feels it.
  3. Keep the streak, watch it grow. From baby, to young adventurer, to a grown-up with a bamboo crown.

Cringe to Star

iPhoneConfidence

Scared of the camera? Same. Let's fix that, one tiny mission a day.

How it works

  1. Pick your character. They start out Shy, hiding behind their phone, like most of us.
  2. Do one small mission a day. It starts with practice in a private camera nobody sees, and builds up to posting.
  3. Grow from Shy to Star. A 180-day path through Trying, Confident and Creator. We measure effort, never fame.

Golden Abs

iPhoneCore

180 days of core training. Your character gets stronger with you.

How it works

  1. Take the plank test. One quick hold picks your level: beginner, intermediate or advanced.
  2. Do a short workout a day. 8 to 12 minutes with follow-along demos, and your phone's camera counts your reps. Rest days count too.
  3. Go from Wobbly to Golden. Earn XP and keep your streak while your character grows from Wobbly Beginner to Golden Core.

Posture Panda

MacFree

Sit up straight, without having to think about it.

How it works

  1. Calibrate once. Sit the way you want to sit and press a button.
  2. It watches your posture. It tracks your head and shoulders right on your Mac. Slouch for 8 seconds and a banner pops up.
  3. It reminds you to move. After 45 minutes of sitting, time to stand up and stretch.

Fix My Face

MacFree

Unclench. Your face carries more tension than you think.

How it works

  1. Learn your relaxed face. Calibrate once with a neutral expression.
  2. It spots the frown. It notices lowered brows and a scrunched nose while you work.
  3. A friendly nudge. Hold the frown and a banner reminds you to let go. It lives quietly in your menu bar.

I used to be an office guy.

For 17 years I had the badge, the lanyard and the calendar full of meetings. Trade marketing, buying for one of Latin America's biggest retailers, running retail and partnerships at a bank. Good jobs. Big spreadsheets. And a lot of great ideas that never got built.

Then I moved to Vancouver and decided I was done waiting for permission. One person with a laptop and some stubbornness can ship a lot more than you think.

Now I just build the ideas that pop into my head. A panda that keeps your habits alive. A star that helps you face the camera. A character who trains abs with you. An app that tells me to sit up straight. Neural Studio is where those ideas live, and where I help you build yours.
